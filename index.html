Snowflake Analytics Platform for Sales and Operations

An ELT platform on Snowflake that brings ERP, CRM and file-based data into one governed warehouse for reporting. Azure Data Factory lands source extracts in Azure Data Lake, Snowpipe loads them into a RAW layer, and dbt models clean and conform the data into STAGING and a star-schema MARTS layer. Streams and Tasks handle incremental (CDC) loads, role-based access and masking policies protect sensitive columns, and Power BI semantic models serve the dashboards.
